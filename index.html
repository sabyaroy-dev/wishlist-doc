Introduction

This Help Centre is your complete guide to Lonigma Wishlist — the all-in-one wishlist app for Shopify. Whether you have just installed it or you're ready to unlock the advanced features, these guides take you through every step.

From installation and theme setup to design, sharing, alerts, integrations and analytics, every feature you can use today is documented here — so you can build, launch and scale a wishlist that turns saved items into sales.

Who it's for
Shopify store owners and staff
Plan required
Free to start
Time to complete
15 minutes for the quick start

Why choose Lonigma Wishlist?

Effortless engagement. Shoppers save items in one tap from product pages, collection pages, search results and home-page carousels — no account, no sign-up, no interruption to their browsing. The heart places itself in your theme, so there is no code to write. Guest wishlists work on every plan, and the moment a shopper logs in, their saved items follow them to every device.

Recover lost sales. When a shopper moves to remove something from their cart, offer to save it for later instead of losing it. They can move items between cart and wishlist freely, or save an entire cart in a single click — keeping their interest alive when they aren't ready to check out yet. Starter and above.

Turn intent into purchases. Alert shoppers automatically when a wishlisted product comes back in stock or drops in price — the two moments a saved item most often becomes a sale. Send by email, SMS or WhatsApp, with quiet hours and sending limits so you stay welcome in the inbox. Connect Klaviyo to add scheduled wishlist reminders and summary digests. Email alerts: Starter and above · SMS and WhatsApp: Growth and above.

Data-driven insights. See exactly which products get saved, which of them convert, and which sit ignored — top wishlisted products, top converting products, save-for-later leaders, your most wishlist-active customers, and the revenue your alerts recovered. Use it to prioritise restocks, plan campaigns, and stop guessing at demand. Export any of it to CSV. Full analytics and exports: Growth and above.

Fully customisable. Match your store's brand natively — colours, button shapes, icons, layouts, and the exact position of every control on every surface. Start from a style preset to get close in one click, then fine-tune. Upload your own SVG icons or write scoped custom CSS when you need pixel control. Custom SVG and custom CSS: Starter and above.

Built to be shared. Every wishlist can be shared across seven channels with full privacy controls, so shoppers send their saved items to the person actually buying. Each shared link brings a new visitor to your store with a ready-made basket — and campaign tags let you measure exactly what that traffic is worth.

GETTING STARTED › Theme setup
▦

Add the wishlist to your theme

Place wishlist controls on product pages, collections, the cart, and a dedicated wishlist page.

Enable the app embed

Go to Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds. Turn on Wishlist Appearance and save.

Product pages

Open Storefront → Product page to control whether the wishlist action appears, where it is placed, and what shoppers see before and after saving a product.

1. Choose visibility and placement

Enable Show Add to wishlist on product pages to make the action available. If your theme does not contain the Lonigma product-page app block, enable Auto-render and choose whether the button appears before or after Add to cart or near the price.

The preview updates as you change placement. Review both Desktop and Mobile to confirm the wishlist action is visible without interrupting variant selection or checkout controls.

2. Set the button presentation

Choose whether the action displays an icon, a label, or both. Write short labels for the two states: the invitation shown before saving and the confirmation shown after the product is added.

For most stores, Icon + label, Add to wishlist, and Added provide the clearest experience. The global colours and icon selected under Brand & visual style are applied automatically.

3. Select one placement mode

App block is recommended because it gives you visual placement control in Shopify’s theme editor. Use Auto-render only when the block is not installed and the theme uses a standard product form. Reserve the Developer hook for custom templates that need exact code-level positioning.

Avoid duplicate buttons

When the product-page app block is present, turn off Auto-render. Using both placement methods can display two wishlist actions.

Collection and browse (PLP)

Open Storefront → Collection & browse to configure wishlist controls on product-listing pages. These settings apply to product cards rather than the full product-detail page.

1. Select the storefront surfaces

Enable the product-card heart independently for collection pages, the home page, search results, product recommendations, and cart recommendations. Turn on only the surfaces your theme actually uses so the storefront stays consistent.

2. Set desktop and mobile placement

Choose where the action appears on desktop product cards. Overlay placements sit on the product image; below-title and below-price placements use the card’s content area. Leave Mobile placement on Inherit desktop placement, or override it when a corner control feels crowded in a two-column mobile grid.

3. Choose the card action and variant behavior

Select an icon-only, label-only, or icon-and-label presentation for product cards. Compact grids usually benefit from an icon, while wider cards can support a short label without competing with product information.

When variant selection is enabled, saving a multi-variant product opens a small picker so the shopper can choose a size or colour. Disable it when you prefer a faster one-tap save that does not select a particular variant.

4. Choose the right collection mode

Use an overlay for image-led grids, or place the action below the title or price when badges and quick-add controls already occupy the image. Custom product-card snippets can use the developer hook for exact placement.

No separate collection app block is required

Collection actions are rendered by the Wishlist Appearance app embed using the surface and placement settings above.

WISHLIST SETTINGS › Display modes
▣

Choose how the wishlist opens

Configure how shoppers open, name, organise, and act on their saved products across every wishlist surface.

Name the wishlist experience

Open Storefront → Wishlist. Choose the shopper-facing noun used in labels, then set the page title, first-list name, and initial sort order. These values should use the same language your store already uses for favourites or saved products.

The shopper noun changes action labels such as “Add to wishlist.” The default list name is separate and is used when a customer’s first list is created.

Choose the wishlist shell

Select Side drawer, Modal, or Dedicated page. For a dedicated page, confirm the storefront path, choose the initial Tiles, List, or Compact layout, and decide whether shoppers may switch layouts themselves.

Side drawer

Recommended for most stores. It keeps shoppers on the current page while giving fast access to saved products.

Modal

A centred overlay that works well for focused, compact wishlists.

Dedicated page

Best for larger lists and content-rich designs. Shared links point to this page.

Launcher and naming

Enable multiple named lists when shoppers need separate collections for occasions, people, or projects. Use the plan default for the maximum list count unless your store needs a stricter limit.

Place success and undo messages with the Toast position setting. Configure the floating launcher independently, including its corner, icon/label treatment, and optional custom label.

Configure item actions and retention

Choose whether Add to cart keeps the product in the wishlist or moves it out. Style the primary cart action separately from secondary controls such as Remove and the alert bell.

Inactive-list cleanup is optional. Set the number of inactive days after which lists are removed, or use 0 to keep saved lists indefinitely.

Review retention before enabling cleanup

Automatic cleanup affects wishlist and save-for-later lists. Choose a period that matches your store’s customer-service and retention policies.

WISHLIST SETTINGS › Branding

Match the wishlist to your brand

Create one consistent visual system for every wishlist surface. Work through the controls below in order, checking the live preview before you save.

Open Brand & visual style

From the Lonigma Wishlist app, open Storefront, then select Brand & visual style. The other tabs control individual storefront surfaces; this tab sets the shared colours, button treatment, and icons used across all of them.

Start with a preset

Select Minimal, Balanced, Bold, or Custom as your starting point. A preset applies a coordinated group of defaults; you can then adjust individual values without rebuilding the design from scratch.

Set brand colours

Enter your button background, button text, icon, and link colours as hexadecimal values. Use the live preview to check contrast, hover states, and how the design reads beside your product content.

Preview before saving

Check both Desktop and Mobile previews. Make sure text remains readable and the primary wishlist action is easy to identify.

Choose button and icon shapes

Match the wishlist controls to your theme by choosing a button style and corner radius. Then assign different symbols to Wishlist and Save for later so shoppers can distinguish the two actions immediately.

Heart is the most familiar wishlist symbol. Bookmark, Pin, or Clock work well for save-for-later because they communicate a separate action without competing with the wishlist heart.

Advanced customisation

Starter and higher plans can upload separate custom SVG icons for Wishlist and Save for later. Upload a small, single-colour SVG; Lonigma sanitises and optimises it, then uses the configured icon colour so it stays consistent across the storefront.

Use scoped custom CSS only when the standard controls cannot produce the required result. Keep those overrides narrow so theme changes do not unexpectedly affect wishlist components.

Cart and save for later

Give shoppers several ways to preserve purchase intent without losing the contents of their cart.

Add cart items to a wishlist

Open Storefront → Cart & save for later. Enable the per-line Add to wishlist action to copy an individual cart item into a named wishlist while leaving the original item in the cart.

Enable Save cart to wishlist when shoppers should be able to copy every current cart item in one action. Choose Auto placement for standard themes, or use the cart app block or developer hook for exact positioning.

Enable Save for later

Save for later moves the item out of the cart and into a private system-managed list. Enable the feature switch before configuring its cart-line controls.

The remove-flow option intercepts deletion and offers a lower-friction alternative: shoppers can remove the item permanently or move it to Save for later instead.

Configure the cart page and drawer

The full cart page can show saved items as a horizontal strip beneath cart lines. The cart drawer uses a compact module that can remain collapsed so it does not compete with checkout. Configure and preview these surfaces independently.

Understand what each action does

Add to wishlist copies one item and keeps the cart unchanged. Save for later moves one item out of the cart. Save cart to wishlist copies every cart item and leaves quantities and cart contents unchanged.

Choose labels that make the difference clear

Shoppers should understand whether an action copies or moves an item before they select it.

Custom CSS

Add focused, brand-specific polish to Lonigma Wishlist surfaces without editing your Shopify theme code.

Use the visual controls first

Presets, brand colours, icon choices, button styles, and placement settings are safer to maintain. Use Custom CSS only when those controls cannot produce the finish you need.

Open Advanced (Custom CSS)

In Lonigma Wishlist, open Storefront → Advanced (Custom CSS). Add CSS in the editor, review the byte counter, and use the live preview to confirm that the Wishlist Appearance app embed is active.

Target stable wishlist selectors

Style Lonigma-owned classes such as .wl-heart, .wl-button, .wl-share-menu, and .wl-shell-page. These selectors are designed for wishlist surfaces and are less likely to collide with the merchant theme.

Avoid broad theme selectors such as product-card, cart-item, or section wrappers. Those classes can be renamed by a theme update or unintentionally restyle unrelated storefront content.

Know which properties are safe

CategoryExamplesGuidance
Cosmeticcolor, background, border, border-radius, font, opacity, paddingGenerally safe for brand polish.
Motiontransition, transformUse subtle values and preserve accessibility.
Structuraldisplay, position, overflow, width, height, z-index, pointer-eventsUse cautiously; these can hide, detach, or block wishlist controls.

Start with a small recipe

Change one surface at a time, then test the unsaved, saved, hover, and keyboard-focus states. The following examples use only app-scoped selectors.

Rounded heart

.wl-heart {
  border-radius: 999px;
  transition: transform 150ms ease;
}

.wl-heart:hover {
  transform: scale(1.04);
}

Branded primary action

.wl-button[data-style="primary"] {
  border-radius: 10px;
  letter-spacing: .02em;
}

Larger mobile target

@media (max-width: 640px) {
  .wl-heart { padding: 10px; }
}

Validate before saving

Check product pages, collection cards, the wishlist drawer or page, cart actions, sharing menus, and saved-state messages on desktop and mobile. If the editor warns about a risky property, confirm that the rule cannot hide a functional control before keeping it.

Keep a recovery path

Save a copy of working CSS before a larger change. If the storefront layout breaks, remove the newest rule or use Reset custom CSS, then rebuild in smaller steps.

Analytics and exports

See what shoppers save, what converts, and where wishlist activity creates revenue.

Analytics overview

Use the date-range selector to review wishlist activity for a specific reporting window. The Overview tab brings retention, engagement, conversion, revenue, trends, and sharing performance together.

Data retention

Choose how long raw customer-level analytics are retained. Aggregate reporting history remains available beyond the selected raw-data period.

Engagement and saves by surface

Track active wishlist owners, wishlist additions, total saved-product value, and save-for-later activity. Surface cards show where shoppers saved products, such as the home page or collection pages.

Conversion and revenue

Measure how wishlist additions progress to cart and purchase, then compare wishlist-attributed revenue and average wishlist value.

Trend chart

Follow additions, cart moves, purchases, and revenue across the selected date range. Hovering a point reveals the value recorded for that day.

Share funnel

Review how shared wishlists progress from shares to clicks, cart additions, and purchases. Expand inactive channels when you need channel-level detail.

Product performance

Compare top-wishlisted products, top-converting products, and save-for-later leaders.

Choose a reporting period to compare additions, moves to cart, save-for-later actions, purchases, revenue, and conversion for each product. The top-converting table requires at least five additions before a product qualifies.

Customer activity

Find active customers and understand how saved products move toward purchase.

Use the engagement filters to isolate customers who received alerts, shared a wishlist, moved an item to cart, or completed a purchase. Each row compares additions, cart movement, purchases, revenue, conversion, and engagement signals for the selected period.

Exports

Export available reports to CSV for analysis and campaign workflows.

Create one-time exports or schedule recurring S3 delivery by data kind, frequency, and reporting range. Pending exports remain in the queue; completed exports show their row count, expiry time, and a download action.

Connect Klaviyo

Send wishlist activity to Klaviyo and build flows around shopper intent.

Klaviyo setup guide

Follow the in-app checklist to create a private API key, enable synchronization, choose the events and triggers you need, and send a test event before building flows.

Connection and events

Open Integrations → Klaviyo, enable Klaviyo sync, and paste a private API key with event and profile write access. A masked preview confirms that a key is already on file without revealing it.

Need help finding your key? Where to find Klaviyo Private API key?

Wishlist events

Choose which shopper actions create Klaviyo metrics. Core events include additions, removals, save for later, moves to cart, shared-link clicks, and wishlist-item purchases.

Synced metrics and profile data

Review the metrics and profile properties Lonigma sends to Klaviyo. The integration covers core wishlist activity, product-state triggers, reminders, alert engagement, wishlist item count, item IDs, and primary-wishlist details.

Product triggers

Enable product-state triggers for back-in-stock, low-stock, and price-drop activity. Set the minimum available stock, low-stock threshold, and minimum percentage decrease so metrics are created only when the conditions match.

Revenue automation

Growth adds engagement metrics for wishlist views, scheduled reminders, summary reminders, and alert delivery. Configure reminder delay and summary cadence, then enable the alert sent, opened, and clicked events you want to use.

Flow setup

Build Klaviyo flows using the matching Lonigma metric as the trigger. Suggested flows cover wishlist welcome, reminders, summaries, back in stock, low stock, price drop, and save-for-later recovery.

Advanced operations

Inspect the dead-letter queue and replay failed Klaviyo synchronization jobs when delivery problems occur. When the queue is healthy, the panel confirms that there is nothing to replay.

Activity and testing

Use the Activity panel to confirm the last successful sync, last failure, and last test event. The event history records status and time, while Send test event verifies the connection before you launch a flow.
